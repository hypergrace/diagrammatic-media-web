Cello + Electronics

(2014-2018)

#experimental-music, #ambient, #live-processing, #max-msp, #live-electronics, #sound

category: performance Between 2014 and 2018, I performed extensively in improvisatory and semi-improvisatory settings with amplified cello live processed with electronics in MaxMSP.

Tired Hearts Kick Darkness and Bleed Light (2017)

is an audience absorbing interrogation of intimacy. The quartet transforms space through light, movement, and sound—juxtaposing textures, tempos, angles, and qualities/quantities of touch.

Credits Britta Joy Peterson (choreography, dance); Juan Coel Rodriguez (dance); Evan Christian Anderson (light)
