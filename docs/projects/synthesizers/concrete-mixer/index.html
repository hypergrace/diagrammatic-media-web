Concrete Mixer

four-voice stereo eurorack synthesizer audio/cv mixer (2024)

#synthesizers, #analog-electronics, #ccam, #eurorack, #workshop, #ccam

category: synthesizers

Screenshot 2026 04 05 211917

I designed a four-voice stereo audio/control voltage mixer for the eurorack setup. I developed this for the Futurhythmachines workshop at the Eskenazi Museum of Art on April 17th 2026 with CCAM.

The mix, send, receive, and distortion circuits are based on Moritz Klein's mono mixer.

The LED amplitude displays are based on Starling's Sync module.
