Lanterns is a digital-physical responsive light and multi-channel sound instrument I developed during my PhD to probe the dynamics of collaboration between groups of people with inorganic, moving matter. This work generated performances, workshops and scholarship.

The system was activated in collaboration with dancer/choreographer Britta Joy Peterson.

The work was hosted by Synthesis Center at ASU, where it was shown publicly in workshops and performances. We performed with the system at MOCO 2017 at Goldsmiths in London.

I designed the physical devices, and composed the responsive algorithmic sonic and lightning behaviors. I used hacked an Arduino into salvaged GameTrak controllers to sense approximately the physical movement vectors and speed of the lanterns. Arduinos sent OSC over ethernet to a computer running MaxMSP, which spatialized sound driven by speed, acceleration, and each lantern's physical proximity to one another. Max also sent lighting behaviors out over DMX to the lantern's dimmerpacks.

materials: GameTrak controllers, theatrical grid mounts, Arduino microcontrollers, Arduino ethernet shields, network router, computers, 8-16.2 channel sound system, Max/MSP code, DMX lighting controller network interface, DMX dimmer packs, dimmable LED lightbulbs, custom light sockets, power cabling, and lighting plugs.
