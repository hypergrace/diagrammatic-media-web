CCAM Estuary is a reprogammable eurorack synthesizer module built on the Daisy Patch SM microcontroller. It features:

- 4 CV outs
- 2 CV Ins
- 2 Gate Outs 
- 2 Gate Ins 
- Stereo Audio In
- Stereo Audio Out 
- 8 Potentiometers
- 8 LEDs
- 2 3-way switches

I designed the PCB and layout of this synthesizer for CCAM's Estuary Group electronic music collective in Fall 2024.

The group led a workshop at [Hairpin Arts Center](https://ccam.world/programs/25-03-29-estuary-group-workshop/) in March of 2025 with firmware designed by Garnett Grimm. Garnet and Bonnie designed [this fantastic zine](https://ccam.world/resources/estuary-system-manual/) describing the firmware they wrote for that workshop. 

I revised the design of the modules, wrote new firmware, added a 4-channel stereo mixer module for a set of workshops and activations in 2026 and 2027: 

- April 2026 // [Indiana University Bloomington, Eskenazi Museum of Art](https://ccam.world/programs/26-04-17-ccam-estuary-indiana-university/), Bloomington, Indiana
- November 2026 // [Public Space One (PS1), Iowa City, Iowa](https://www.publicspaceone.com/events/making-electricity-musical-with-modular-synthesis)
- January 2027 // Graduate Design Studio @ Iowa State University, Ames, Iowa 

I created a zine for this new system which [you can access here](/assets/pdf/frm-zine-estuary-june.pdf).
