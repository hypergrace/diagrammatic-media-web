CCAM Earth is a reprogammable desktop synthesizer built on the Daisy Seed microcontroller. It features:

- 2 CV outs
- 2 CV Ins
- Gate Out 
- Gate In 
- Stereo Audio In
- Stereo Audio Out 
- 8 momentary Buttons
- 8 LEDS
- 6 Potentiometers

I designed the PCB and layout of this synthesizer for CCAM's Futurhythmachines: Chicago House programming in Spring 2024. With Juan Flores, I wrote three firmwares that allowed for groups to devise their own Chicago House music performances.
