I designed a four-voice stereo audio/control voltage mixer for the eurorack setup. I developed this for the Futurhythmachines workshop at the Eskenazi Museum of Art on April 17th 2026 with CCAM. 

The mix, send, receive, and distortion circuits are based on Moritz Klein's [mono mixer](https://www.ericasynths.lv/service/file/download/product_id/1161/file_id/614/). 

The LED amplitude displays are based on [Starling's Sync module](https://starling.space/via/sync/documentation).
